# Changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added
- **211 new KPIs (271 in total).** Library entries cover finance, sales, marketing, customer service,
  digital/SaaS, operations, supply chain, procurement, HR, IT & security, risk & compliance, strategy
  and ESG, plus industry sets for banking, insurance, telecom, retail, healthcare, energy, public
  sector, hospitality, airlines, education, construction, real estate, pharma, media and non-profit.
  Each has a definition, formula, worked example, purpose, BSC perspective, leading/lagging type,
  level, functions, industries and relationships to other KPIs.
- New industries (Hospitality, Airlines & Aviation, Education, Construction & Engineering, Real
  Estate, Pharma & Life Sciences, Media & Entertainment, Non-profit) and a Sustainability & ESG
  function, all searchable and filterable.
- New references: Accelerate (DORA metrics), GHG Protocol, GRI Standards, IEEE 1366, PMI Earned
  Value Management, Basel III LCR, Kerzner's project metrics.
- KPI detail marks library entries and explains which sections are documented.

### Fixed
- Search matched short aliases inside other words (e.g. "SPI" inside "hospital"). Aliases now match
  whole words only.
- Industry-neutral searches now prefer cross-industry KPIs, and an industry named in the query
  carries more weight.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-05

### Added
- First offline Android release: KPI library and search, KPI detail, on-device assistant, projects,
  touch strategy map, balance check, executive summary sharing, backup and restore.
